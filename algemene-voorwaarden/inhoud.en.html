For all quotes and assignments of Stevy Vergouwen, trading as Shot by Stevy. Please read them carefully and keep them for later.

1. Applicability

1.1These terms apply to all quotes and agreements of Stevy Vergouwen, trading as Shot by Stevy (KvK 91512840), hereinafter the Contractor.

1.2The Client is whoever gives an assignment to the Contractor, such as an organiser, club, artist or booking agency, a company or a private person.

1.3Deviations only apply if agreed in writing. The Client's purchasing or other terms do not apply.

1.4The Contractor may engage other creatives, such as photographers or assistants. These terms also apply to them.

1.5If the Client is a consumer (a private person not acting in the course of a profession or business), these terms apply only to the extent mandatory consumer law allows. The consumer's statutory rights always remain in force, even if a provision below seems to say otherwise.

2. Quotes and agreement

2.1A quote is valid for 30 days.

2.2The agreement is formed as soon as the Client accepts the quote, in writing or by e-mail, or lets the assignment begin.

2.3The Contractor sends these terms along with the quote. By agreeing, the Client confirms having received them.

2.4Changes to the assignment count as extra work and are only carried out after both parties agree.

3. Prices and payment

3.1All prices exclude VAT and travel costs, unless stated otherwise.

3.2For a fixed price, the Contractor may invoice 50% on agreement and the rest on delivery.

3.3The Client pays within 14 days of the invoice date, without discount or set-off.

3.4After that period the Client is in default without notice. The statutory commercial interest is then due, plus the reasonable costs of collection.

3.5If payment is not made, the Contractor may suspend the work.

3.6If the Client is a consumer, he is only in default after the Contractor has sent a reminder once the period has passed and has given him another 14 days to pay. After that, statutory interest and the legally permitted collection costs are due.

4. Travel costs and extra work

4.1The Contractor charges travel costs at € 0.30 per kilometre driven, per person, return, per working day.

4.2Parking costs and other necessary costs are charged on, after agreeing in advance.

4.3Work outside the quote, such as extra shoots or extra reels, is charged at the prices in the quote or at € 90 per hour, after agreeing in advance.

5. Execution and planning

5.1The Contractor carries out the assignment to the best of his ability. Creative and technical choices lie with the Contractor, within what has been agreed.

5.2Shoot days are planned in consultation. The Client provides access, information and material in time. If the assignment is delayed as a result, the dates shift accordingly.

5.3Delivery times are target dates, not fixed deadlines. If exceeded, the Contractor is first given a reasonable period in writing to still deliver.

6. Delivery, revisions and storage

6.1The Contractor delivers via a download link. The first version follows within 2 weeks of the last shoot day, unless the quote states another period. Fast delivery, for example an aftermovie or reel within a few days of an event, can be agreed and may carry a surcharge, always stated in the quote in advance.

6.2Two rounds of revisions are included, with feedback bundled per round. An extra round is extra work.

6.3The Contractor keeps the files for 60 days after delivery. After that they may be deleted. The Client arranges his own backup.

7. Copyright and use

7.1The copyright in all photos, footage and edits remains with the Contractor, unless agreed otherwise in writing.

7.2After full payment the Client receives a right of use of 3 years for online publication, social media and his own website. Paid advertising is not included and is agreed separately. Other use, such as print, television or advertising by third parties, requires prior written agreement and may cost extra.

7.3The Client may not resell, transfer or license the work to third parties, and may not use it to train AI systems.

7.4The Contractor may show the work in his portfolio, on his website and on social media, in consultation with the Client and without confidential information.

7.5It is appreciated, but not required, that the Client credits © @stevyvergouwen when publishing.

7.6For use outside the agreement the Client owes a fee of 2 times the usual rate for that use. The Contractor retains the right to full damages.

7.7For an artist, DJ or booking agency, the right of use also covers their own promotion, such as a press kit, booking material, flyers and posters for their own shows. Use by organisers, labels, sponsors or other third parties is not covered and is agreed separately.

8. Original files

8.1Original footage (RAW files) is only delivered if stated in the quote, or for a separate fee.

8.2The Client may adapt the work for the agreed use, such as cropping or shortening. A substantial edit requires the Contractor's prior approval.

9. Location, people on camera and music

9.1The Client provides safe access to the location and states in advance which safety rules apply, such as mandatory protective equipment. The Contractor follows those rules. Safety comes before the shot.

9.2Where needed, the Client arranges permission from recognisable people who appear on camera, and states what must not be visible or readable, such as drawings and screens. At events, clubs and festivals, where visitors cannot give permission one by one, the Client ensures that visitors are informed in advance that photos and video are being made, for example with a notice at the entrance or in the ticket terms. If a visitor does not want to be on camera, the Contractor takes this into account as far as reasonably possible.

9.3Claims from third parties, such as people shown, about the Client's use of the work are at the Client's expense. The Client indemnifies the Contractor, except in case of intent or gross negligence.

9.4Footage may contain music that does not belong to the Contractor, such as sets, tracks and background music. The rights to that music remain with the rights holders, such as the artist, the label and Buma/Stemra. The Client ensures that use of that music in the delivered work is permitted and arranges permission or licences where needed.

9.5The Contractor is not liable for the blocking or removal of the work, or for claims from platforms or rights holders, about music in the work, except in case of intent or gross negligence.

10. Cancelling and rescheduling

10.1Cancelling or rescheduling is only possible in writing. An e-mail is enough.

10.2When cancelling or rescheduling a shoot day: free of charge more than 14 days in advance. Within 14 days, 50% of the price for that day is due. Within 48 hours, 75% is due.

10.3If a day has to move because of the event's programme or the weather, we plan a new date in consultation. Cancellation costs then do not apply, provided this is reported 48 hours in advance.

10.4If the Client cancels the assignment while it is in progress, he pays for the work already done and the costs already incurred.

10.5If the Client is a consumer, the costs in 10.2 apply to the extent they are reasonable and legally permitted. For a distance agreement, a consumer may withdraw within 14 days without giving a reason. If the consumer asked to begin within that period, he pays for the part already carried out.

11. Liability and force majeure

11.1The Contractor is only liable for direct damage caused by a shortcoming attributable to him.

11.2Liability is limited to the invoice amount of the assignment concerned.

11.3The Contractor is not liable for consequential damage, such as lost revenue, nor for damage from use of the work outside the agreement.

11.4These limitations do not apply in case of intent or deliberate recklessness.

11.5In circumstances beyond the Contractor's control, such as illness, malfunction, extreme weather or an inaccessible location, the Contractor may suspend the assignment or propose new dates, without compensation. If this lasts longer than 30 days, either party may terminate and the work carried out is paid for.

11.6If the Client is a consumer, the limitations in this article apply to the extent the law allows.

12. Complaints

12.1The Client reports a complaint about the work or the invoice in writing and with a description within 14 days of delivery or the invoice date. After that the work is considered approved.

12.2A complaint does not suspend payment.

12.3If the complaint is justified, the Contractor repairs or replaces the work within a reasonable period. If that fails, a proportionate discount follows.

12.4A consumer reports a defect within a reasonable time after discovering it. For him, the right to complain does not lapse after 14 days.

13. Final provisions

13.1Dutch law applies. Disputes go to the competent court in the district where the Contractor is established, currently the court of Zeeland-West-Brabant.

13.2If a provision is void or annulled, the other provisions remain in force. The parties then replace it with a valid provision that comes as close as possible.

13.3In writing includes e-mail.

13.4In case of differences between the Dutch and the English text of these terms, the Dutch text prevails.
